## Source Notes

- Figures 1--4 are deterministic SVG diagrams rendered to PNG. This keeps
  labels, arrows, and scientific semantics editable and reproducible.
- Fig. 1 is the selected fused layout: it preserves the original paper's
  input--Harness--VLA--runtime--robot execution narrative while replacing
  outdated Transition/Critic/cache labels with the final Monitor, VLM Planner,
  typed gate, recovery, and profile-admission contracts. The compact
  `fig1_framework_v2` variant is retained as a non-primary alternative.
- Figure 5 is generated by `render_fig5_results.py` from
  `generated/fig5_libero10_data.csv`.
- The raw LIBERO rollout directories were removed during an earlier cleanup.
  Figure 5 therefore reproduces the retained submitted table rather than
  claiming a new raw-log aggregation.
- The completed 185/200 LIBERO result evaluates the deterministic precursor
  Harness (Transition, priors/memory, and Critic/Retry). It is not attributed
  to the later event-triggered VLM Planner path.
- Figures 6--8 and the earlier supplementary taxonomy are not referenced in the
  current manuscript. They remain archived because the robosuite pilot,
  single-run diagnostic, and former taxonomy do not strengthen the main
  evidence chain.

## Visual Contract

- Orange: Agentic supervision and recovery decisions.
- Green: semantic planning, memory, and robot execution states.
- Blue: replaceable VLA policy.
- Purple: Optimize Runtime and deployment-profile control.
- Red: rejection, safe hold, or stop.
- Solid black: observations and learned action flow.
- Dashed black: feedback and traces.
