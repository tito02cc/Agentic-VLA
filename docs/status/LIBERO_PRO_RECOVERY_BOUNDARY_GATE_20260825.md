# LIBERO-Pro Recovery Boundary Gate

Date: 2026-08-25

## Purpose

The first Fixed Recovery batch exposed a state-boundary defect: observations
generated by the recovery skill remained in the short-horizon
`ExecutionRiskMonitor` window. The next routing decision could therefore treat
the recovery itself as a continuing policy stall and immediately safe-stop.

The affected formal outputs were preserved under:

- `_diagnostics/warmup6_false_positive/`;
- `_diagnostics/warmup60_pre_recovery_monitor_reset/`.

They are diagnostics and are excluded from the formal aggregate.

## Correction

After a registered recovery primitive completes, the runner now:

1. resets the monitor's short-horizon command, proprioceptive and visual
   windows;
2. discards pending risk produced inside the recovery primitive;
3. seeds a new low-risk observation for the next VLA primitive.

The recovery budget, task evaluator, VLA checkpoint, policy noise, action
horizon and success definition were unchanged.

## Paired Regression Gate

The corrected runtime was rerun on all ten initial states of the three nominal
tasks that previously contained successful-Frozen regressions.

| Task | Success | Recovery episodes | Safe stops | Control steps |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| LIBERO-10 T4 | 10/10 | 1 | 0 | 2,259 |
| LIBERO-10 T6 | 10/10 | 3 | 0 | 2,166 |
| LIBERO-10 T7 | 10/10 | 1 | 0 | 2,679 |
| Total | 30/30 | 5 | 0 | 7,104 |

All five trajectories that invoked recovery subsequently completed the task.
The gate artifacts and videos are stored in
`results/libero_pro_recovery_reset_gate_20260825/`.

## Decision

The corrected recovery boundary passed the no-regression gate. The complete
four-suite, three-method study may proceed. These gate results are engineering
validation and are not pooled into the formal benchmark table.
